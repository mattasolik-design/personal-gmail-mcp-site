Privacy Policy

Last updated October 30, 2026.

Personal Gmail MCP is a private tool used by one person, Matt Solik, with his own Google account. It isn't offered to the public.

What it accesses

With the account owner's permission through Google sign-in, it reads messages and creates drafts in that owner's Gmail. It has no ability to send email.

How data is used

Email content is used only to answer the account owner's own requests on his own computer. The sign-in token is stored locally on that computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
